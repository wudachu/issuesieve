# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in IssueSieve a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, intimidation, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Until a dedicated conduct address is published, report incidents privately through the repository owner's public contact channel. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
